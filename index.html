🥗 The 5-Part Recipe for Perfect Prompts

Whenever you talk to any AI model, include these five ingredients:

🎭
Role
Who the AI acts as (e.g., "Act as an IIT professor").
🎯
Task
The specific action to do (e.g., "Explain Newton's 3rd law").
📖
Context
Your background (e.g., "I am a 12th CBSE student preparing for exams").
📐
Format
How to present it (e.g., "Use a table with bullet points").
⛔
Limits
Rules to avoid fluff (e.g., "Under 200 words, simple tone, give examples").
